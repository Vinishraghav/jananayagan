How It Works

  1. Upload your video to a public cloud host (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, AWS S3, etc.)
  2. Get the direct URL to your video file (must be publicly accessible)
  3. Paste the URL into the input field above and click "Load video"
  4. Share the page with others — they can play your video instantly

FAQ

Why can't GitHub host my 6.33GB video?

GitHub's file size limits are much smaller. Most platforms limit individual files to a few GB. External hosting services are designed for large video files.

What video formats are supported?

The HTML5 video player supports MP4, WebM, and Ogg formats. MP4 is the most widely compatible.

Is my video URL secure?

Your video URL is only stored in the URL bar. It is not sent to any server or tracked on this site.

Can I embed this on my website?

Yes! Use an iframe with: https://your-domain.com/jananayagan?v=YOUR_VIDEO_URL
